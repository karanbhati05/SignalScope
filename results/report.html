Experiment interpretation

Randomized synthetic demonstration: absolute difference in conversion rates with a normal-approximation interval and unpooled Wald test. No real lift claim. A real experiment needs prespecified sample size, assignment checks, guardrails and no repeated peeking. Sentiment is a lexicon baseline; sarcasm, slang and mixed sentiment remain failure modes. Clusters are descriptive, not causal.
